What is happening

Reverse index: written once, read cheaply

When a block closes, every key is written into the buckets of its top 2 anchors (rows). A bucket holds only a few keys, so extras overflow and are dropped from that bucket (red count). Outlined cells are the ones this query opens. Diamonds are the per-anchor summary tokens: a coarse average of everything stored under that anchor.
